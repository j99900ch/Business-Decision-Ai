Business DecisionAI

Business DecisionAI is an AI-powered business decision support application designed to help users analyze business questions and make more informed, data-driven decisions. The system combines Generative AI, RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), LangChain, and business-context analysis to provide structured insights rather than simple text-based answers.

Users can enter a business decision or question, provide relevant business information, and receive an AI-generated analysis covering key factors, potential risks, opportunities, reasoning, and recommended actions.

🚀 Key Features
🤖 AI-Powered Decision Analysis – Converts business questions into structured decision insights.
📊 Business Context Analysis – Considers provided business information while generating recommendations.
🔎 RAG Integration – Retrieves relevant information from available knowledge sources before generating responses.
🦜 LangChain Integration – Supports the retrieval and AI-processing workflow.
🧠 Gemini-Powered Intelligence – Uses Google Gemini for natural-language reasoning and response generation.
⚠️ Risk & Opportunity Analysis – Highlights possible risks, benefits, and important decision factors.
💡 Actionable Recommendations – Provides practical recommendations instead of only describing the problem.
🎙️ Voice Interaction – Designed with speech input/output capabilities for more natural interaction.
🖥️ Interactive Streamlit UI – Simple interface for interacting with the decision-support system.
🏗️ Technology Stack

Frontend / UI: Streamlit
LLM: Google Gemini
AI Framework: LangChain
RAG: FAISS + document retrieval
Language: Python
Environment: Python Virtual Environment + .env configuration
